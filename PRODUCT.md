# Product

Inferred from the hackathon brief; the user asked not to be interviewed.

## What it is

A visual digest of one personal-injury case file, read live and read-only from Clio Manage. It shows the story of the case first, where existing tools (Clio, CasePeer, Lawmatics) show counts and lists with the story one click away.

## Who uses it, and the job

- **Attorney (firm view).** Up to speed in 90 seconds without knowing what to ask: who the client is, what the case is worth against the coverage behind it, what needs action, and how the case got here. Then dig into anything, with every number traceable to its Clio record.
- **Treating provider on a lien (provider view).** Four answers at a glance without emailing the firm: is the case alive, is there coverage, is my patient showing up, what does the firm need from me. Nothing about strategy, value or other providers.
- **Client (client view).** Reassurance in plain English: where the case is, what happens next, the next appointment, what to bring, and why the take-home amount depends on liens.

## Principles

- Story first, lists second.
- A source behind every number and date; derived and AI values marked as such.
- Access boundaries enforced in the data layer, never by hiding elements.
- Honest empty states; no fabricated values.
- Motion explains (orient, confirm, show change, reveal hierarchy) and never slows anyone down.

## Surfaces

`/` is the Front Page, the one design of the product (mode: Operate for the firm, Read for provider and client).
